About

The problem is rarely the technology.

Most IT organizations aren't struggling because of a technology gap. They're struggling because of how work flows — how it arrives, gets prioritized, handed off, and resolved.

Over 27 years across enterprise, MSP/VAR, and fractional executive environments, I've led teams through the kind of structural and operational change that produces lasting results — not just short-term fixes. I bring a framework-driven approach grounded in ITIL 4, LeanOps, and Team Topologies, with the leadership track record to take an organization from diagnosis to execution.

Equally fluent in the language of engineers and executives. Equally comfortable on the whiteboard and in the boardroom.
